        Frequently Asked Questions

        Everything you need to know about deliveries, sizes, returns, and ordering with BRO OUTFIT.

        1. How long does handover & delivery take?
        Orders placed before 2 PM IST are dispatched on the same business day. Delivery across major metro cities takes 2 to 4 business days. You will receive live WhatsApp and SMS tracking updates as your parcel moves.
        2. What is your return & exchange policy?
        We offer a hassle-free 7-day exchange and return policy for unworn items with original tags intact. You can initiate a return or size exchange directly through your account or by WhatsApping our concierge at +91 9510859808.
        3. How do I know my correct size?
        Each product page includes our Atelier Interactive Size Guide with precise chest, waist, and length measurements in inches. If you fall between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a modern relaxed drape or contacting concierge for custom advice.
        4. What payment methods are accepted? Is COD available?
        Yes! Cash on Delivery (COD) is available nationwide. We also accept all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking with zero extra transaction fees.
        5. Are the fabrics 100% genuine and pre-shrunk?
        Every single garment is woven from certified pure Mulberry silk, high-thread count French linen, or shuttle-loomed selvedge denim. Fabrics undergo sanforization and garment-washing to prevent post-wash shrinkage and color bleeding.
        6. Where is BRO OUTFIT physically located?
        Our flagship studio and fulfillment atelier is located near Sattbazar, Veraval, Gir Somnath, Gujarat. Patrons are welcome to experience our bespoke tailoring lounge by booking an appointment.

        Still have questions?

        Our Ahmedabad styling and concierge team is available 24/7 on WhatsApp & Call.
